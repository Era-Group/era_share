Key Features

🔄

Every tender, delivered to you

Fresh opportunities from Forsah and Etimad arrive automatically, so you never have to check the portals again. Your notes, tags and decisions stay safe with every update, and closed tenders are tucked away rather than lost.

🗂️

Decide fast, with a clear flow

Move each tender through a simple path — review, worth studying, not for us, won — with one-click buttons and colour-coded badges. Everyone sees exactly where each opportunity stands, so nothing slips through the cracks.

✨

The best-fit bids, front and centre

Tell us what your company does, once. From then on every tender gets a relevance score from 0 to 100, colour-coded so the strongest matches jump out instantly. Stop scrolling endless lists and go straight to the bids you can actually win.

🎯

From tender to deal in one click

Spotted a winner? Turn it into a live CRM opportunity in a single click — the right contact and a follow-up reminder are set up for you, with no risk of creating a duplicate. Your pipeline stays clean and your team never drops the ball.

Find the right tender in seconds

Slice the list any way you need — by status, match score, closing date, city, government body, award method or tags — and focus on exactly what matters today. The opportunities you care about are always one quick filter away.
