Era CRM — Forsah & Etimad Tenders

Government tender pipeline for CRM, with AI business matching

خط أنابيب المناقصات الحكومية في CRM مع مطابقة النشاط بالذكاء الاصطناعي

Automatically imports tender opportunities from the Forsah and Etimad portals and turns them into a triage pipeline of studyable tenders — scored by AI against your company's business and converted into CRM opportunities in one click.

Key Features

🔄

Automatic tender feed

Scheduled import from the Forsah and Etimad portals. Forsah records are upserted by reference, so triage status, tags and linked opportunities survive every refresh; expired tenders are archived, not deleted.

جلب مجدول من منصتي فرصة واعتماد، مع حفظ حالة الفرز والوسوم والفرص عند كل تحديث.

🗂️

Studyable-tenders triage

A clear workflow — To Review → Studyable → Not Suitable → Converted — with one-click buttons, a status bar, coloured badges, search filters and group-by on both tender lists.

سير عمل واضح للفرز: بحاجة لمراجعة ← قابلة للدراسة ← غير مناسبة ← تم التحويل.

✨

AI business matching

Describe your business once in Settings; the AI scores how relevant each open tender is (0–100, colour-coded) and surfaces the best fit. Built on Odoo's standard AI agent framework — use any configured provider.

يقيّم الذكاء الاصطناعي مدى صلة كل مناقصة بنشاط شركتك (٠–١٠٠) ويُبرز الأنسب.

🎯

One-click to opportunity

Convert a promising tender into a CRM opportunity with the right source, contact and a follow-up activity — linked back to the tender, with a duplicate guard.

حوّل المناقصة الواعدة إلى فرصة في CRM بنقرة واحدة مع نشاط متابعة.

🔎

Rich search & grouping

Filter by study status, business match, closing date, city, company, method, tags and more; group and sort to focus on what matters today.

فلاتر وتجميع غنية: الحالة، المطابقة، آخر موعد، المدينة، الجهة، التعميد والوسوم.

🌐

Fully Arabic UI

Complete Arabic translation across fields, menus, buttons, filters, tooltips, scheduled actions and messages.

واجهة عربية كاملة لكل الحقول والقوائم والأزرار والرسائل.

How it works

1

Tenders are imported automatically from the portals.

2

Set your business description in Settings → CRM.

3

AI scores relevance; triage and convert the best.
