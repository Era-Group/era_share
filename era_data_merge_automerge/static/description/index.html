Production-Safe Execution

Designed with safety first: processes duplicates in controlled batches, logs every merge action, and skips groups with merge conflicts.

Background Processing

Runs silently in the background on a configurable schedule — no disruption to users or ongoing operations during the merge process.

Merge Logging

Every auto-merge action is logged for audit purposes — administrators can review what was merged, when, and with what outcome.
