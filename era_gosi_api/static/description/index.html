How it works

  1. Connect your GOSI account once — a quick, guided setup, done in minutes.
  2. Open any employee — their national ID is all you need on file.
  3. Click Verify GOSI. That's the whole job.
  4. Seconds later: status, contributions, and coverage dates — right there on the profile.
  5. Any mismatch? Your payroll team hears about it before the month closes, not after.
