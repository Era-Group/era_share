How it works

  1. Connect your GOSI account once — a quick, guided setup.
  2. Open any employee and make sure their national ID is filled in.
  3. Click Verify GOSI.
  4. In seconds, status, contributions, and coverage dates appear — done.
  5. Your payroll team gets a heads-up on any mismatch before the month closes.

كيف يعمل

  1. اربط حساب GOSI مرة واحدة — إعداد سريع وموجَّه.
  2. افتح أي موظف وتأكّد من إدخال رقم هويته.
  3. اضغط التحقق من GOSI.
  4. في ثوانٍ تظهر الحالة والاشتراكات وتواريخ التغطية — انتهى.
  5. يصل فريق الرواتب تنبيه بأي فارق قبل إغلاق الشهر.

Your sensitive GOSI access is encrypted and never shown again once saved. Every check uses a fresh, short-lived connection that's closed the moment it's done — nothing sensitive is left sitting around. Simple, secure, and worry-free.

An active GOSI subscription and each employee's national ID or IQAMA number on file — that's it. Pair it with payroll to unlock automatic contribution checks, and you're catching discrepancies before they ever cost you.
