AI CV Match Score

The AI reads the applicant's CV and scores it against the job description — giving a 0–100 match score with a written summary of fit and gaps.

⚡

One-Click Analysis

Trigger AI analysis from the applicant form or list view — results appear on the record without leaving Odoo.

Objective Ranking

Sort and filter applicants by AI score — quickly identify the top candidates without reading every CV manually.
