How it works

  1. Flip it on for the teams you choose — that's your part done.
  2. They open Odoo in as many tabs as they like. It sorts itself out.
  3. A call arrives and rings in exactly one place — never two.
  4. They answer calmly. Clean audio, nothing competing.
  5. The call ends; the line steps back and goes quiet.
  6. A tab closes mid-day? Another takes over in seconds. No one even notices.
